To install, use your browser’s Install App menu. On iPhone or iPad, choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

Listening together

On Chrome, connect to a Nest speaker or an existing Google Home speaker group. Use Google Home to create groups and adjust their playback and volume.

On Safari on an Apple device, use AirPlay or Control Center to select HomePods. For multiple HomePods, choose your AirPlay 2 speakers in Control Center.

Google and Apple speakers use separate systems. This app cannot combine them into one synchronized group, or become a Google Home voice music service.

Remote listening

Listen here from anywhere. To control another player, start a listening room on that device and share its remote link. Keep that player awake and connected. Anyone with the link can control playback. Rooms expire after 24 hours without activity.

Speaker selection happens on the player, on the speakers’ local network. Remote controllers can then change songs, playback and volume.

Your music

Eight finished songs were imported from SoundBreak. Add further songs using their direct SoundBreak MP3 links. The SoundBreak connection does not provide this website with an automatic library sync. Added songs, playlists, favorites, history and colors are saved in this browser.
